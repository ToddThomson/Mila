# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Mila is an open project, and everyone who takes part in it — contributors, maintainers, and
people opening issues or discussions — deserves a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
experience, regardless of background, identity, or level of experience.

This Code of Conduct is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is the reference standard for the expectations below.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in code reviews, issues, and discussions.
- Assume good faith, and prefer clarifying questions over accusations.
- Give and accept feedback graciously, keeping it focused on the work rather than the person.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experience levels; this is an educational project and
  newcomers are explicitly welcome.

## Unacceptable behavior

Conduct that makes the project unwelcoming or unsafe is not tolerated. This includes
harassment of any kind, personal or demeaning attacks, deliberate intimidation, sustained
disruption of discussions, publishing others' private information without consent, and any
other behavior that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
and discussions — and whenever an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Mila is maintained by a single author. To report a concern, email **todd.thomson@me.com**.
Reports will be reviewed in good faith and kept as confidential as is practical.

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying these standards and may take any action deemed
appropriate in response to a violation, including warning the responsible party, removing
contributions, or blocking further participation. Concerns are handled on a best-effort basis
for this volunteer-maintained project.
