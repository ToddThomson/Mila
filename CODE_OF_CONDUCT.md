# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Mila is an open, source-distributed project maintained by a single author. The goal
of this document is simple: keep participation in the project a welcoming,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or experience
level. It applies to everyone who takes part — the maintainer included.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews.
- Assume good faith, and give newcomers room to learn — this project deliberately
  favors readable, understandable code, and questions are welcome.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully; critique the code, not the person.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments, and discriminatory language
  or imagery.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained interruption of discussion.
- Publishing another person's private information (such as a physical or email
  address) without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — the repository, issues,
pull requests, and discussions — and also when an individual is representing the
project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Mila is maintained by a single author. If you experience or witness behavior that
violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately by email to
**todd.thomson@me.com**. Reports are handled confidentially and in good faith.
As a solo, volunteer-maintained project there is no formal response-time
guarantee, but you can expect a good-faith acknowledgement.

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may
take any action deemed appropriate, including editing or removing contributions
(comments, commits, code, issues, and other content) that violate this Code of
Conduct, and warning, temporarily restricting, or permanently banning any
contributor for behavior considered inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
